About Us

The Cultural Club of the Department of English

Chatok is the official cultural club of the Department of English, Jagannath University — run by and for the department's own students, across every year and batch. We exist to give the department a shared cultural life alongside its academic one.

Through the year we organise literary sessions and reading circles, drama and recitation, competitions tied to major literary anniversaries, and department-wide festivals — spaces where students engage with literature and art beyond the syllabus.

The name itself carries the spirit of the work: in Bengali and Sanskrit poetry, the chatak is a bird said to drink only raindrops caught mid-air, refusing river, pond, or well however thirsty it grows — a classic symbol of devotion that will not settle for less than the real thing.

চাতক পাখি

"The chatak bird waits with beak open to the sky, taking no water but the rain — a devotion old poets returned to again and again as a symbol of a thirst only the right thing can answer."

In Their Words

Voices of Chatok

"Culture is not merely something we preserve; it is something we live, create, and pass forward. Through Chatak, we hope to build a community where creativity is encouraged, diversity is celebrated, and every young mind feels inspired to leave its own mark."

ড. তানিয়া তাহমিনা
Chairman, Department of English

"To me, Chatak is more than a cultural association—it is a space where young minds find their voices, creativity takes shape, and art brings us together. Through music, literature, drama, art, and ideas, Chatak gives every student a place to express their stories, talents, and dreams. May Chatak continue to inspire creativity, celebrate culture, and give young dreams their wings."

মো: আশরাফুল ইসলাম
President, Chatok

"Chatok has always felt like my own little child—something I've watched grow through love, sacrifice, and countless struggles. Looking back, every struggle feels worth it. I hope those who come after us will love, nurture, and carry this little legacy even further."

আয়শা সিদ্দিকা
